What the Street Team Is For

That is what the Oathbound Street Team is for.

This is a crew for anyone who wants to help bring more noise, more bodies, and more energy into the room. Whether you are in one of our tour cities, nearby, or just want to help spread the word from wherever you are, you can be part of making these shows hit harder.

What the Street Team Does

The street team helps get the word out before each show.

That can mean sharing flyers online, posting tour dates, inviting friends, tagging local bands and venues, dropping the ticket link in the right places, printing flyers, asking local shops if they will hang one, or simply making sure the people who would love this show actually know it exists.

You do not need to be a promoter. You do not need a huge following. You do not need to know the "right" people.

You just need to care about heavy music, local scenes, and helping bring the right people into the room.

Keep It Simple

  • Only post flyers where allowed.
  • Don't cover other bands' flyers.
  • Don't spam people.
  • Tag the band when you share.
  • Be cool to venues, bands, and other fans.

Why It Matters

Oathbound is built for the room.

The sweat. The feedback. The first hit of the drums. The stranger next to you screaming the same line. The moment the whole floor moves like one living thing.

But people cannot show up to a show they never hear about.

That is where you come in.

If you have ever wished more tours came through your city, more local bands got seen, more people cared about the scene, or more heavy music had a fighting chance outside the algorithm, this is one way to help.

Join the street team. Share the noise. Bring your friends. We'll see you in the pit.
