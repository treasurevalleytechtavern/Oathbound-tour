Join the Oathbound Street Team

Hard shows do not happen in a vacuum. They happen because someone shared the flyer, tagged their friends, woke up the group chat, and told the people who would care where to be.

Why It Matters

This is for anyone who wants to help make these shows feel bigger before the first note hits.

Whether you are in a tour city, nearby, or spreading the word from somewhere else, you can help bring more noise, more bodies, and more energy into the room.

Heavy music still grows person to person: one flyer, one share, one invite, one friend who almost missed the show until somebody told them. More people in the room means more support for the scene, more fuel for every band on the bill, and a better night for everyone who shows up.

How You Help

You help get the word out before each show.

That can mean sharing flyers online, posting tour dates, inviting friends, tagging local bands and venues, dropping the ticket link where people will actually see it, printing flyers, or asking local shops if they will hang one.

You do not need to be a promoter. You do not need a huge following. You just need to care about heavy music, local scenes, and helping the right people find the show. Show up early, be cool to the venue, stay for the other bands, and bring good pit energy.

Perks

We Want to Show Love Back

Street Team members may be eligible for guest list spots, free or discounted merch, stickers, promo packs, and other thank-you perks when available.

Perks can vary by city and show, but if you're helping us spread the word, we want to show love back where we can.

Why It Matters

Oathbound is built for the room.

But people cannot show up to a show they never hear about.

That is where you come in.

If you have ever wished more tours came through your city, more local bands got seen, more people cared about the scene, or more heavy music had a fighting chance outside the algorithm, this is one way to help.
